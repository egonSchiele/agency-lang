The telephone hums, a mystic refrain
As words I speak, transform in its domain
From English to French, to Spanish, to more
A magical device that opens many doors

The airwaves pulse, with a musical beat
As the world comes alive, at my feet
I dial a number, in any land
My voice reaches out, beyond the sand

A new horizon, a never-ending sea
Of cultures, and histories, that I see
Through the eyes of the other, I learn to know
A world, that was once hidden, in shadows

The telephone, a bridge, between us all
A magical device, that brings us closer, to stand tall.
