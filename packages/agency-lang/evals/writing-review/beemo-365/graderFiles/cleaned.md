With online banking you can securely manage your finances from the comfort of your own home. Account balances can be checked, funds transferred and bills paid using a smartphone or computer.

A particularly useful feature of online banking is the ability to set up alerts for your accounts.  Notifications are sent via email or text message when a large purchase is made or when a deposit is received.  

Another benefit is availability of account information at any time. You don't have to wait a bank to open or worry about business hours. Detailed account statements and transactions are readily available.

Online banking can also be more cost-effective. Many banks offer free online banking services so you can eliminate postage costs by paying bills online.
